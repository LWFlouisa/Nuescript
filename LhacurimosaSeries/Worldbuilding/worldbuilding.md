# Ethnic Groups
This ethnic group started out darker skinned toned, but gradually became lighter with eyes that became more yellow by the decade. They primarily live on wooden platforms filled with sand and gravel, subsisting primarily as fisherman. And avoid sub liminal spaces and ancient ruins with ancient mineshaffs.
